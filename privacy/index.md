# Learning Tails World Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Learning Tails World is a children’s learning app designed for ages 3–5. We built the app to work without advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics.

## Information the app handles

Learning Tails World stores the following information locally on the family’s device:

- the selected animal avatar;
- completed adventures and learning progress; and
- local app preferences.

This information is used only to remember the child’s experience. It is not sent to our servers or shared with third parties.

## Information we do not collect

The app does not collect names, birth dates, precise locations, contacts, photos, voice recordings, advertising identifiers, or analytics data. It does not contain advertising or third-party tracking technology.

The app’s spoken directions are prerecorded and included with the app. Learning Tails World does not use the microphone.

## Accounts and internet access

Version 1.0 does not require or create an account. Its learning activities work offline after installation.

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads, crash reports, and other service data under Apple’s own privacy policy. We do not receive child profiles from Apple.

## Deleting local information

A grown-up can open the parent-gated Grown-up Area and choose **Delete Local App Data**. Deleting the app from the device also removes its locally stored gameplay information.

## Children’s privacy

Learning Tails World is designed to minimize data use for young children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not allow children to communicate with other users.

## Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is released.
